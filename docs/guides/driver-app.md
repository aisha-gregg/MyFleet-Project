# MyFleet Driver App user guide

The MyFleet Driver App lets you log in to a vehicle, see your jobs, complete pre-trip checks, and review your driving.
**Version:** 4.2 · **Platforms:** iOS 16 and later, Android 11 and later

## Sign in

1. Open the Driver App.
2. Enter the email address and temporary password from your fleet manager.
3. Create a new password when asked.

!!! note
    Forgot your password? Select **Forgot password** on the sign-in screen. If you have no email access, ask your fleet manager to reset it.

## Log in to a vehicle

To link your trips to you, log in to the vehicle before you drive.

=== "With a driver ID"
    Hold your driver ID against the reader on the dashboard. The reader beeps twice and the app shows the vehicle name.

=== "In the app"
    1. Select **Vehicles**.
    2. Select the vehicle's registration number, or scan the QR code on the windscreen sticker.
    3. Select **Log in**.

## Complete a pre-trip check

Your fleet may require a pre-trip check before your first trip of the day.

1. On the home screen, select **Start pre-trip check**.
2. Check each item and select **OK** or **Defect**.
3. For a defect, take a photo and add a short description.
4. Select **Submit**.

**Result:** your fleet manager gets the report straight away. If you reported a defect that makes the vehicle unsafe, don't drive. Wait for your fleet manager's instructions.

## View your jobs

Select **Jobs** to see today's jobs in order. Select a job to see the address, contact, and notes. Select **Navigate** to open your maps app.

When you arrive, select **Arrived**. When you finish, select **Complete** and collect a signature if the job needs one.

## Check your driving score

Select **My driving** to see a score from 0 to 100 based on:

- harsh braking
- harsh acceleration
- speeding
- idling

Tap any score to see the events that lowered it, with time and location.

## Privacy

When you log out of a vehicle, or turn on **Private mode** (if your fleet allows it), the app stops linking trips to you.
Your fleet manager can see your trips, jobs, and score, but not your phone's location outside work.
