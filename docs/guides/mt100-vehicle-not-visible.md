# MT-100 vehicle does not appear in the MyFleet Dashboard

**Applies to:** MyFleet MT-100 · **Article type:** Troubleshooting
**Article ID:** KB-0142 · **Last reviewed:** 9 November 2026 (Release 4.2)

## Overview

If your vehicle does not appear in the MyFleet Dashboard after installing the MT-100 tracker, follow the troubleshooting steps below to identify and resolve the issue.

## Possible causes

The vehicle may not appear on the platform for the following reasons:

- The MT-100 tracker is not properly connected to the vehicle's diagnostic port.
- The tracker is not receiving power.
- The tracker has not established a cellular network connection.
- The data transmission period has not elapsed.
- The vehicle is not associated with the correct customer account.

## Troubleshooting steps

### 1. Check the tracker connection

Ensure that the MT-100 is firmly connected to the vehicle's diagnostic port.

### 2. Check the power indicator

Check the tracker's LED indicator.
A green LED indicates that the tracker is receiving power.

If the green LED is not illuminated, check that the vehicle ignition is switched on. If the indicator remains off, contact Technical Support.

### 3. Check the cellular network connection

Check the tracker's LED indicator.
A blue LED indicates that the tracker has established a cellular network connection.

If the blue LED is not illuminated, verify that the tracker is properly connected and receiving power. If the issue persists, contact Technical Support.

### 4. Allow time for data transmission

Once the cellular network connection is established, allow up to five minutes for vehicle data to appear in the MyFleet Dashboard.
After five minutes, refresh the Dashboard and check whether the vehicle is visible.

### 5. Verify the customer account

Check that the vehicle is associated with the correct customer account.
If you cannot verify the account association, contact your fleet administrator or Technical Support.

## Contact Technical Support

If the vehicle still does not appear in the MyFleet Dashboard after completing the troubleshooting steps, contact Technical Support through the approved support channel.

To help the support team investigate the issue, provide:

- Vehicle identification or fleet reference.
- MT-100 tracker identification, if available.
- Description of the issue.
- Current LED indicator status.
- Installation date and time.
- Relevant screenshots of the MyFleet Dashboard.

!!! note
    Include photographs of the tracker installation where relevant. Only take photographs when it is safe to do so.
